# Billing & Service Contracts

## 1. Service Contract Tiers

### Tier 1 — Basic Maintenance Contract

| Feature | Details |
|---------|---------|
| Price | NGN 45,000 per visit (residential), NGN 85,000 per visit (commercial) |
| Frequency | Quarterly (4 visits per year) |
| Includes | Oil change, oil filter, air filter inspection, battery check, general inspection |
| Does Not Include | Spare parts beyond filters, emergency callouts, major repairs |
| Billing | Invoiced per visit or annually in advance (10% discount for annual prepayment) |

### Tier 2 — Comprehensive Maintenance Contract

| Feature | Details |
|---------|---------|
| Price | NGN 150,000/year (residential ≤30kVA), NGN 350,000/year (commercial ≤200kVA) |
| Frequency | Quarterly scheduled visits + 2 emergency callouts per year |
| Includes | All filters, oil, belts, basic spare parts, labor, emergency response within 24 hours |
| Does Not Include | Major components (AVR, alternator, engine rebuild), fuel |
| Billing | Annual invoice, payable upfront or in 2 installments |

### Tier 3 — Premium All-Inclusive Contract

| Feature | Details |
|---------|---------|
| Price | NGN 500,000/year (≤100kVA), NGN 1,200,000/year (≤500kVA), custom quote (>500kVA) |
| Frequency | Monthly scheduled visits + unlimited emergency callouts |
| Includes | All parts, labor, emergency response within 4 hours (Lagos), remote monitoring, annual load bank test |
| Does Not Include | Fuel, structural modifications, damage from acts of God |
| Billing | Annual or semi-annual invoicing |

## 2. Billing Cycles and Payment Terms

### Invoice Schedule

| Contract Type | Invoice Issued | Payment Due |
|--------------|----------------|-------------|
| Per-visit contracts | Within 3 business days after service | 14 days from invoice date |
| Annual contracts | At contract start date | 30 days from invoice date |
| Emergency callouts (no contract) | At time of service | Immediate / 7 days from invoice date |

### Accepted Payment Methods
- Bank transfer (GTBank, First Bank, Access Bank — details on invoice)
- Online payment via Paystack (link provided on invoice)
- Cheque (corporate accounts only, requires 5 business days clearance)
- Cash (at service centers only, receipt issued immediately)

## 3. Common Billing Issues and Resolution

### 3.1 Duplicate Charge

**Customer says:** "I was charged twice for the same service visit."

**Resolution Process:**
1. Ask customer for both invoice numbers and dates
2. Cross-reference in the billing system — check if two invoices exist for the same visit date and service address
3. If confirmed duplicate: issue a credit note for the duplicate amount within 3 business days
4. Refund will be processed via the original payment method within 7-10 business days

**Root Cause Prevention:** The billing system should enforce one invoice per service visit per contract. Escalate to the billing system team if duplicates are a recurring issue.

---

### 3.2 Incorrect Amount Charged

**Customer says:** "The invoice amount does not match my contract rate."

**Resolution Process:**
1. Pull the customer's contract and verify the agreed rate
2. Compare with the invoiced amount
3. If overcharged: issue a credit note for the difference
4. If undercharged: inform the customer and issue a corrected invoice (do not back-bill for previous undercharges unless the contract specifies)
5. Update the billing system with the correct rate to prevent recurrence

---

### 3.3 Invoice for Service Not Received

**Customer says:** "I received an invoice but no one came to service my generator."

**Resolution Process:**
1. Check the service log — was a technician dispatched? Was a service report filed?
2. If no service report exists: the visit did not happen. Cancel the invoice immediately.
3. If a service report exists but customer disputes: contact the technician for confirmation and check GPS logs
4. Reschedule the missed service visit within 5 business days
5. Follow up with the customer to confirm satisfaction

---

### 3.4 Contract Renewal Dispute

**Customer says:** "My contract was auto-renewed and I was charged without consent."

**Resolution Process:**
1. Check the original contract terms — does it include an auto-renewal clause?
2. Check if a renewal reminder was sent (should be sent 30 days before expiry)
3. If no reminder was sent: offer full refund of the renewal charge
4. If reminder was sent but customer did not respond: offer pro-rated refund for unused period
5. Cancel the auto-renewal and update customer preference

---

### 3.5 Warranty Repair Being Billed

**Customer says:** "I was charged for a repair that should be covered under warranty."

**Resolution Process:**
1. Verify warranty status — is the product within the warranty period?
2. Check if the fault qualifies for warranty coverage (see Warranty Policy)
3. If covered: reverse the charge and reclassify as warranty repair
4. If not covered (e.g., wear item, customer-caused damage): explain the exclusion with reference to the warranty terms
5. If disputed: escalate to the warranty manager for review
