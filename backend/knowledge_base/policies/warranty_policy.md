## 2. What Is Covered

The warranty covers defects in materials and workmanship under normal use conditions:

- Engine manufacturing defects
- Alternator/generator head defects
- Control panel electronic component failures
- Structural and canopy defects
- Wiring harness defects
- Cooling system component defects (radiator, water pump, thermostat)

---

## 3. What Is NOT Covered

The following are explicitly excluded from warranty coverage:

### Consumable / Wear Items
- Engine oil and oil filters
- Fuel filters and air filters
- Drive belts and hoses
- Batteries (separate battery warranty applies)
- Fuses and bulbs
- Gaskets and seals (unless related to a manufacturing defect)

### Customer-Caused Issues
- Damage from overloading beyond rated capacity
- Use of contaminated or incorrect fuel
- Use of non-genuine or non-approved spare parts
- Modifications or alterations not authorized by Mikano
- Damage from improper installation (not performed by Mikano or authorized installer)
- Damage from failure to follow the Operation and Maintenance Manual
- Damage from power surges, lightning strikes, or flooding

### Operational Neglect
- Failure to perform scheduled maintenance as outlined in the maintenance schedule
- Operating the generator without adequate ventilation
- Operating with known faults (e.g., continuing to run with low oil warning)
- Failure to maintain a maintenance log

---

## 4. Conditions That Void the Warranty

The warranty is immediately void if:

1. **Unauthorized repairs** are performed by persons not authorized by Mikano
2. **Non-genuine parts** are used for repairs or maintenance
3. **Maintenance records** cannot be provided upon request (must show scheduled maintenance was performed on time)
4. **Serial number plate** is removed, altered, or illegible
5. **The product is resold** without notifying Mikano and transferring the warranty
6. **Environmental damage** — submersion in water, exposure to corrosive chemicals, or operation in conditions outside the rated specifications

## 6. How to File a Warranty Claim

### Step 1: Gather Documentation
- Original proof of purchase (invoice or receipt)
- Product serial number (on the nameplate)
- Maintenance log showing all scheduled services were performed
- Description of the fault, including when it first appeared and under what conditions

### Step 2: Contact Mikano Customer Care
- Phone: 07001234567
- Email: warranty@mikano-intl.com
- Online: service.mikano-intl.com/warranty
- Visit: Any Mikano service center (Lagos, Abuja, Port Harcourt, Kano)

### Step 3: Inspection
A Mikano-authorized technician will inspect the product to determine:
- Whether the fault is a manufacturing defect (covered)
- Whether the fault is caused by misuse, neglect, or wear (not covered)

### Step 4: Resolution
If the claim is approved:
- Defective parts will be replaced with genuine Mikano parts at no charge
- Labor for the repair is covered
- If the product cannot be repaired, a replacement unit of equivalent specification will be provided

### Typical Processing Time
- Initial response: 2 business days
- Inspection: 3-5 business days after contact
- Repair/replacement: 5-10 business days after inspection approval

## 7. Maintenance Requirements for Warranty Validity

To keep the warranty valid, the following minimum maintenance must be documented:

| Interval | Required Maintenance |
|----------|---------------------|
| Every 250 hours or 6 months | Oil change, oil filter replacement |
| Every 500 hours or 12 months | Fuel filter replacement, air filter inspection, belt inspection |
| Every 1,000 hours or 24 months | Full service including coolant flush, valve clearance check, injector inspection |
| Every use | Visual inspection for leaks, check oil and coolant levels |

**Records must include:** Date, running hours at time of service, work performed, parts used, and name of person/company who performed the service.
