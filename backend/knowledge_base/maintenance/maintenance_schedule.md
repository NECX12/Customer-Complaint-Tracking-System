# Maintenance Schedule — Mikano Power Equipment

## Overview

Regular maintenance is the single most important factor in extending equipment lifespan and preventing costly breakdowns. All Mikano generators, inverters, and electrical panels require scheduled servicing as outlined below.

---

## Weekly Checks (Owner / Site Operator)

These checks should be performed by the site operator or equipment owner every 7 days, even if the equipment has not been used.

### Generator Weekly Checklist

- **Engine oil level** — Check with the dipstick while the engine is cold. Oil should be between the MIN and MAX marks. Top up with the manufacturer-recommended grade if below MIN.
- **Coolant level** — Inspect the radiator overflow tank. Coolant should be at the FULL mark. Use premixed 50/50 antifreeze/water solution. Never open the radiator cap while the engine is hot.
- **Fuel level** — Ensure adequate diesel is available. Mikano recommends maintaining at least 50% tank capacity at all times to avoid air ingestion.
- **Battery terminals** — Inspect for corrosion (white/green powder). Clean with a wire brush if needed. Check that connections are tight.
- **Battery voltage** — Measure with a voltmeter. A fully charged 12V battery should read 12.6V or above. Replace if consistently below 12.0V.
- **Air filter** — Visual inspection only. Check for obvious blockage or damage.
- **Leaks** — Walk around the unit and look under the base frame for oil, coolant, or fuel leaks.
- **Control panel** — Power on the control panel briefly and check for error codes or warning lights.
- **Security** — Verify that enclosure doors/panels are secure and that no unauthorized modifications have been made.

### Inverter Weekly Checklist

- **Battery bank voltage** — Check individual battery voltages. All batteries should be within 0.3V of each other. Imbalanced batteries indicate a failing cell.
- **Ventilation** — Ensure the inverter room has adequate airflow. Operating temperature should be below 40°C.
- **LED indicators** — Check that all status LEDs show normal operation (green). Amber or red LEDs indicate faults.
- **Connections** — Visually inspect all cable connections for signs of overheating (discoloration, melting).

## 250-Hour Service (Certified Technician Required)

This service should be performed by a Mikano-certified technician every 250 running hours, or every 3 months — whichever comes first.

### Generator 250-Hour Service

- **Engine oil change** — Drain old oil, replace oil filter, refill with manufacturer-specified grade. Record the running hours at the time of service.
- **Oil filter replacement** — Always replace the oil filter at every oil change. Use genuine Mikano/OEM filters only.
- **Fuel filter inspection** — Check for water contamination and debris. Replace if discolored or partially blocked.
- **Air filter cleaning/replacement** — Remove and clean the air filter element. Replace if damaged or excessively dirty. Foam pre-filters should be washed and re-oiled.
- **Belt tension check** — Check alternator and fan belts for correct tension and wear. Replace belts that show cracking, glazing, or fraying.
- **Coolant condition** — Test coolant concentration with a refractometer. Concentration should be 45-55%. Top up or replace if degraded.
- **Battery load test** — Perform a load test on the starting battery. Replace if it cannot hold above 9.6V under load for 15 seconds.
- **Electrical connections** — Tighten all terminal connections on the alternator, battery, and control panel.
- **Running test** — Start the generator and run at 50% and 100% load for 15 minutes each. Verify stable voltage (±5%) and frequency (50Hz ±0.5Hz).
- **Update maintenance log** — Record all actions taken, parts replaced, running hours, and technician name.

### Inverter 250-Hour Service

- **Battery equalization** — Perform equalization charge on lead-acid battery banks. This is not applicable to lithium batteries.
- **Terminal torque check** — Re-torque all DC and AC terminal connections to manufacturer specifications.
- **Fan/filter cleaning** — Clean or replace cooling fans and intake filters.
- **Firmware check** — Verify the inverter firmware version. Update if a newer version is available from Mikano.

## 500-Hour Service (Full Overhaul)

This service includes everything from the 250-hour service plus the following additional items.

### Generator 500-Hour Service

- **Fuel filter replacement** — Replace both primary and secondary fuel filters.
- **Valve clearance adjustment** — Check and adjust intake and exhaust valve clearances per the engine manual.
- **Injector inspection** — Test fuel injectors for spray pattern and opening pressure. Clean or replace as needed.
- **Coolant hose inspection** — Squeeze all coolant hoses. Replace any that feel soft, spongy, or show surface cracking.
- **Exhaust system check** — Inspect the exhaust manifold, gaskets, and muffler for leaks or damage.
- **Vibration damper inspection** — Check anti-vibration mounts for compression or damage. Replace if the generator exhibits excessive vibration.
- **Full electrical test** — Test alternator output, AVR calibration, and generator protection settings with a power analyzer.
- **Load bank test** — Run at 100% rated load for 2 hours. Monitor oil pressure, coolant temperature, voltage, and frequency throughout.

## Annual Maintenance (Comprehensive Inspection)

Performed once per year regardless of running hours. This is a full system audit.

### Generator Annual Maintenance

- All 500-hour service items
- **Coolant replacement** — Drain and replace all coolant. Flush the cooling system.
- **Turbocharger inspection** — Check turbo shaft play, inlet/outlet ducts, and boost pressure (turbocharged models only).
- **Comprehensive load bank test** — 4-hour load bank test at rated capacity. This removes carbon deposits from the engine ("wet stacking" prevention).
- **Fuel tank cleaning** — Drain the fuel tank. Inspect for sludge, water, and microbial contamination. Clean and treat with biocide if necessary.
- **Control panel calibration** — Verify all meter readings against calibrated instruments. Recalibrate if deviation exceeds 2%.
- **Compliance inspection** — Check noise levels, emission readings, and safety systems against current regulatory standards.
- **Report generation** — Produce a comprehensive maintenance report with equipment condition assessment, remaining useful life estimate, and recommended repairs.

### Inverter Annual Maintenance

- All 250-hour items
- **Battery capacity test** — Perform a discharge test to determine remaining battery capacity. Replace batteries below 80% of rated capacity.
- **Surge protection check** — Test all surge protection devices. Replace any that show signs of degradation.
- **Full system commissioning test** — Test automatic changeover, overload protection, and battery-to-mains transition timing.

---

## Maintenance Log Requirements

All maintenance activities must be recorded in the equipment maintenance log book. Each entry must include:

1. Date and time of service
2. Running hours at the time of service
3. Type of service performed (weekly/250hr/500hr/annual)
4. Parts replaced (with part numbers)
5. Observations and recommendations
6. Technician name and Mikano certification number

> **IMPORTANT**: Failure to maintain proper maintenance logs may void the equipment warranty. Mikano reserves the right to request maintenance records when processing warranty claims.

---

## Emergency Maintenance

If any of the following conditions are observed, stop the equipment immediately and contact Mikano service:

- Sudden loss of oil pressure
- Coolant temperature exceeding 105°C
- Visible smoke from the engine compartment (not exhaust)
- Unusual metallic noises from the engine
- Fuel or oil leaks near hot surfaces
- Battery acid leaks or swelling
- Burning smell from electrical connections
- Inverter display showing critical fault codes (E01-E05)
