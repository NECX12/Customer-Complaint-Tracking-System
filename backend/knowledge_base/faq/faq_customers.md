## Maintenance Questions

### Q4: How often should I service my generator?

Follow the Mikano maintenance schedule:

| Service Type | Interval | Who Performs It |
|-------------|----------|-----------------|
| Weekly checks | Every 7 days | Equipment owner/operator |
| 250-hour service | Every 250 running hours or 3 months | Mikano-certified technician |
| 500-hour service | Every 500 running hours or 6 months | Mikano-certified technician |
| Annual service | Once per year | Mikano-certified technician |

> **Tip**: Even if your generator runs infrequently, perform the weekly checks and the time-based services. Equipment that sits idle without maintenance deteriorates faster than equipment that is regularly run and serviced.

### Q5: What type of fuel should I use for my diesel generator?

Use clean, filtered diesel fuel that meets EN 590 or equivalent specifications. Key fuel guidelines:

- **Do not use stale fuel** — Diesel degrades after 6 months. Use fuel within 3 months of purchase for best results.
- **Filter your fuel** — Always pour fuel through a filter funnel to remove dirt and water.
- **Treat stored fuel** — If you store diesel on-site, treat it with a fuel biocide every 3 months to prevent microbial contamination.
- **Never mix fuel types** — Do not add petrol, kerosene, or waste oil to diesel.

### Q6: What engine oil should I use?

Use the engine oil grade specified in your generator manual. General guidelines:

- **Small generators (9–30kVA)**: SAE 15W-40 diesel engine oil (API CI-4 or higher).
- **Large generators (above 30kVA)**: SAE 15W-40 or SAE 10W-30 depending on the engine manufacturer's specification.
- **Always use genuine or approved oil brands.** Using substandard oil voids the warranty.
- **Change oil at every 250-hour service** or sooner if the oil appears very dark or gritty.

### Q7: Can I perform maintenance myself, or do I need a Mikano technician?

- **Weekly checks** — Yes, you can and should perform these yourself. They are simple visual and level checks.
- **All other services (250hr, 500hr, annual)** — These must be performed by a Mikano-certified technician to maintain your warranty. Unauthorized servicing by unqualified mechanics may void your warranty.

### Q8: My generator has been sitting unused for several months. What should I do before starting it?

Before restarting a generator that has been idle for more than 2 months:

1. Check the engine oil level and condition. Change if it looks dark or milky.
2. Drain any water from the fuel filter/separator.
3. Check the fuel quality — if the fuel has been in the tank for more than 6 months, drain and replace it.
4. Charge or replace the starting battery.
5. Check the coolant level.
6. Inspect for any pest damage (rodents may chew wires and hoses).
7. Run the generator at no load for 10 minutes, then gradually apply load.

If the generator does not start after these checks, contact Mikano service.

## Warranty Questions

### Q9: What does the Mikano warranty cover?

The standard Mikano warranty covers:

- **Manufacturing defects** in the engine, alternator, control panel, and cooling system.
- **Period**: 12 months from the date of commissioning or 18 months from the date of delivery, whichever comes first.
- **Coverage**: Parts and labor for repairs of covered defects at a Mikano service center.

The warranty does **NOT** cover:

- Consumables (oil, filters, belts, batteries, fuses)
- Damage from misuse, overloading, or unauthorized modifications
- Damage from operating with incorrect fuel or lubricants
- Normal wear and tear
- Damage from power surges, lightning, or flooding

### Q10: What voids the warranty?

The following actions void the Mikano warranty:

1. **Unauthorized repairs** — Any repair or modification performed by a non-Mikano-certified technician.
2. **Non-genuine parts** — Using aftermarket or non-OEM parts.
3. **Missing maintenance records** — Failure to maintain a proper maintenance log book.
4. **Overloading** — Operating the generator consistently above its rated capacity.
5. **Incorrect fuel or oil** — Using fuel or lubricants that do not meet the specified standards.
6. **Tampering with safety devices** — Disabling or bypassing any protection system.

### Q11: How do I file a warranty claim?

To file a warranty claim:

1. Log into your Mikano customer portal and submit a complaint describing the issue.
2. Include photos or videos of the problem if possible.
3. A Mikano technician will inspect the equipment (on-site or at a service center).
4. If the issue is determined to be a covered defect, repairs will be performed at no charge.
5. If the claim is denied, you will receive a written explanation with the option to appeal.

> **Important**: Keep your maintenance log book up to date. Warranty claims are frequently denied due to missing maintenance records.

## Troubleshooting Questions

### Q12: My generator won't start. What should I check?

Before calling Mikano service, check:

1. **Battery** — Is the battery charged? Check terminal connections for corrosion.
2. **Fuel** — Is there fuel in the tank? Is the fuel shutoff valve open?
3. **Oil level** — Many generators have a low-oil shutdown. Check the oil level.
4. **Emergency stop** — Is the emergency stop button released (pulled out)?
5. **Control panel** — Is the control panel in AUTO or MANUAL mode? Check for error codes.

If none of these resolve the issue, contact Mikano service with the error code displayed on the control panel.

### Q13: My generator is running but there is no power output. What's wrong?

Possible causes:

1. **Circuit breaker tripped** — Check and reset the generator output circuit breaker.
2. **AVR (Automatic Voltage Regulator) failure** — If the voltage meter shows zero or fluctuating, the AVR may need replacement. Do not attempt this yourself.
3. **Loose connections** — Check that all output cables are securely connected.
4. **ATS malfunction** — The Automatic Transfer Switch may not be transferring the load. Check ATS status indicators.

### Q14: My inverter is beeping/alarming. What does it mean?

Common inverter alarms:

| Alarm | Meaning | Action |
|-------|---------|--------|
| Continuous beep | Battery low | Reduce load or wait for mains to restore and recharge |
| Intermittent beep | Overload | Disconnect some appliances to reduce the load below rated capacity |
| Fast beep with red LED | Critical fault | Switch off immediately and call Mikano service |
| No display | No input power or internal fault | Check mains input and battery connections |

### Q15: My generator is producing black/blue/white smoke. Is it normal?

- **Black smoke on startup (brief)** — Normal, especially in cold weather. Should clear within 30 seconds.
- **Persistent black smoke** — Indicates incomplete combustion. Possible causes: dirty air filter, worn injectors, or overloading. Schedule a service.
- **Blue smoke** — Engine is burning oil. Possible causes: worn piston rings, overfilled oil. Requires service.
- **White smoke** — Coolant is entering the combustion chamber. Possible head gasket failure. Stop the generator and call Mikano immediately — this is a serious issue.
